# The MRI pack

The first-party modality pack for MRI: the series classification rules, the roles and the vocabulary that NILS v0 accumulated over years of clinical research data, carried into v1 in meaning rather than by copying code (D12). The pack format is decided by a prototype on the hardest v0 pieces before the second wave of work (C11); until then this directory holds this file.

Packs are AGPL-3.0-only like the engine. Third-party packs for other modalities follow the specification in `contracts/pack/`.
