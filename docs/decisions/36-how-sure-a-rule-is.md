# 36 · How sure a rule is

Opened 2026-09-19. Follows 35, whose S5 made the thresholds honest and thereby exposed this. **Retired 2026-09-23 into [39](39-two-ways-of-knowing-a-stack.md)** ([40](40-the-road-through-wave-6.md) R1): the label model measures each rule's accuracy, and Nima reviews only the outliers it reports. The worksheet stays, as the label model's prior. The sitting below never took place.

## The ask

A rule in the pack writes a confidence when it fires. The body-part rule always writes 0.65 and the base rule always writes 0.70, and the thresholds that decide whether a person is asked are "below 0.65" and "below 0.70". So those thresholds can never fire: every stack they judge sits exactly on the line and is answered without a person. On record 34's corpus that is 573 of 836 stacks, decided by rules whose confidence is a constant.

Three ways out were put to Nima: leave it, move the thresholds so the boundary asks, or have the pack say how sure each rule really is. He took the third.

> Nima, 2026-09-19: "Let's go with your recommendations."

## Why the third

Moving a threshold turns 573 stacks into questions in one step, which is about two thirds of the corpus and close to what v0 flagged. That is not a judgement about any rule; it is a judgement about all of them at once, and it would be made by whoever happened to pick the number.

A confidence is a claim about evidence. "This series description says BRAIN and nothing contradicts it" and "this echo time is short, so it is probably T1 weighted" are not equally strong, and a pack that writes one number for both is not saying anything. The fix is for each rule to say what its own evidence is worth, which is knowledge Nima has and the engine does not.

## How it is done

1. **A worksheet, from the corpus.** For every rule that fired on record 34's corpus: what it decided, how often, on what evidence, the confidence it writes today, and what happened to the stacks it decided. Generated after record 35's wave lands, since that wave changes which rules fire at all.
2. **Nima rules, rule by rule.** Each gets a confidence, or a note that it should never answer alone. This is a conversation, not a batch: a rule at a time, with the evidence in front of him.
3. **A wave applies them**, with the pack's version moved and its own tests, and the corpus re-run to show what the review queue becomes.

## What this will cost, and why that is the point

The queue will grow. A rule honestly marked weak asks a person, and some of those 573 will become questions. That is the outcome, not a side effect: the current silence is not accuracy, it is a test that cannot fail.

What the wave must also show is whether the queue is workable. If the honest numbers produce a queue nobody can get through, the answer is better evidence in the rules rather than a higher threshold, and that is a further record.

## Not in this record

The engine's side of confidence is settled by 35: thresholds read as their words do, one comparison serves every caller, and what the boundary drained is printed. Nothing here changes the engine.
